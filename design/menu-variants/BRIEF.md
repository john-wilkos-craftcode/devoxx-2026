# Main menu variants: the brief

Five agents, five radically different main menus for the Devoxx 2026 cinema page. Each builds an interactive demo in
its own folder. One of them may be ported into the real page, so build as if it will be.

## The job

The cinema page (`site/cinema/`) is a 3D-rendered Kinepolis: you arrive in the hall and its big schedule screen shows
the main menu. Pick a talk and you walk to your seat in that room, where the recording plays and a laptop in your lap
has the notes. During the conference the menu was a day-by-day programme. Now the talks are over, on YouTube and
summarised, so John wants the menu to tell the conference's story:

- **The story.** What the Java community thinks is important now, what it's doing, where it disagrees, the overall
  vibe. Our recap has a headline-length intro and 8 verdicts, each with what everyone agreed on and one debate with
  two sides, the speakers on each side and the talks.
- **What's worth watching now, and why.** Picks with a one-line reason, and honest public signals per talk.
- **How to find what you're interested in.** Search across talks, speakers, tools and topics.
- **The day-by-day programme**, one click away, for people who want it by day, time and room.

**Success criterion (one):** someone seeing it says "wow", and it is still obviously usable as the menu. Usable means:
someone on two hours of sleep finds the story, a talk worth watching and the search within five seconds.

## Mandatory content and functions (real, nothing invented, nothing dropped)

Data: `../data.js` sets `window.MENU` (generated by `make_data.py`; do not edit it). Use it, never hard-code talks.
- `MENU.recap.intro`: the conference in a paragraph. `MENU.recap.trends[]`: 8 verdicts, each with `title`, `body`,
  `count` (talks), `talkIds`, `consensus`, `debate` {`question`, `sides[]` {`stance`, `who[]`, `talkIds`}}.
- `MENU.picks[]`: one provisional pick per verdict {`verdict`, `talkId`, `why`}. Show them as "watch first".
- `MENU.talks[]` (205): `id, title, speakers[{name, company}], room, start, end, type, track, level, minutes,
  keywords, themes, tldr, watchIf, takeaway, videoId, hearts, views, likes, likeRate, thin, seat`.
- Signals, shown honestly: `views` (reach), `likeRate` (likes per 100 views, pulled toward the event mean;
  `thin: true` means under 20 likes, so mark it as "few votes" and never rank on it alone), `hearts` (Devoxx app
  favourites before the talk: interest, not quality). Talks without a video have `views: null`.

Functions every variant must have:
1. **The story**: the intro (or a one-line headline you derive from it, faithful to it) and all 8 verdicts, each
   opening to its consensus and its debate (the question, both sides, who, their talks).
2. **Watch first**: the 8 picks with their reason.
3. **Search**: free text over title, speakers, company, keywords, themes, tldr, track; results update as you type.
4. **Talk list sorting/filtering by signal**: at least views, like rate (with the few-votes caveat) and hearts.
5. **Open a talk**: call `openTalk(id)` (define it in your page; it shows a small toast like "Walking to Room 8,
   seat ready" when `seat` is true, else "Opening the summary"). In the real page this starts the walk.
6. **Programme by day**: an entry that opens a day/time/room view of the talks (Mon 5 to Fri 9 October; times in
   Europe/Brussels). It can be simple, but it must work.
7. **Text version of every talk**: a link to `../../../site/index.html` (the plain text site).
8. A chatbot is NOT required. If you add an "ask" box, it must be an honest guided search over the data, never a fake
   AI answer.

Branding facts: Devoxx Belgium 2026, 5-9 October 2026, Kinepolis Antwerp, 205 talks. The Devoxx wordmark is
"DEVOXX" with the X in orange (#f6921e); you may use the name in type, don't fake a logo file.

## Hard constraints

- **Size.** The menu is a fixed canvas: render it as one element `#menu`, exactly **980 x 613 CSS px** (the hall
  screen, 3.6 x 2.25 m). It is an emissive LCD in a dim rendered hall (the hall glows deep blue around it, the
  current screen background is #0B1440), seen from a few metres away: type must survive that. `?shape=phone` renders
  the phone version instead: **340 x 690 px**, a portrait totem screen. Centre #menu on a dark page for the demo.
  Optional: a "see it in the hall" toggle that shows #menu over `../../../site/cinema/media/hall.jpg` (the screen is
  the middle ~50% of that image's width), for context.
- **Tech.** Self-contained `index.html` (+ your own css/js files in your folder). Plain JS, no build step, no
  framework (the real page is vanilla JS with a small `el(tag, attrs, ...kids)` DOM helper). Pinned CDN libraries are
  allowed only from cdnjs.cloudflare.com, cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm or unpkg.com, with an exact version, and only if they
  earn it (a winner has to be ported). Fonts: Google Fonts allowed. The real page uses Archivo; you may choose
  differently if the concept needs it.
- **Write only in your own folder** (`design/menu-variants/<your-folder>/`). Screenshots go in `<your-folder>/shots/`.

## Quality bars

- No console errors (the shot tool prints them).
- Visible keyboard focus on everything interactive; the whole menu works by keyboard (Tab, Enter, Esc, arrows where
  it makes sense).
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` gives a calm version: no big motion, same content, same functions.
- Nothing stuck invisible if JS fails or is slow: never start content at opacity 0 waiting for an animation; a
  `<noscript>` with the text-version link is fine.
- Readable: body text at least 14 px on the laptop canvas, contrast at least 4.5:1 for text.

## Design discipline

- Load the `frontend-design:frontend-design` skill first (Skill tool) and follow it.
- Ground every choice in the subject: Devoxx, Java, a cinema, a conference that argued about coding agents, the JVM
  and architecture. No decoration without purpose.
- Spend boldness on ONE signature moment. Everything else serves clarity.
- Avoid the AI tells:
  - generic SaaS cards; near-black plus one neon accent; cream + serif + terracotta
  - tracked ALL-CAPS eyebrows everywhere; fade-slide-up on everything
  - one headline word in a different colour; gradients and glows as filler
- Motion that answers the user's input (hover, keys, drag, scroll) is welcome; motion for its own sake is not.

## Process (mandatory)

a. **Plan** (`<your-folder>/PLAN.md`): palette as named hexes, type roles and scale, an ASCII wireframe of the laptop
   canvas and the phone, the signature moment. Check it against the AI-tells list and revise anything generic.
b. **Build** it.
c. **Look at it**: `node tools/ux/shot.mjs design/menu-variants/<your-folder>/index.html design/menu-variants/<your-folder>/shots/iter<N>`
   (run from the repo root; options `--wait ms`, `--hover "<selector>"`). It saves 1-rest, 2-hover, 3-keyboard,
   4-reduced-motion and 5-phone PNGs and prints console errors. Read the PNGs (Read tool). Also open a verdict, run a
   search, sort, open the programme and check those states, e.g. with a small Playwright script of your own in your
   folder (copy the launch lines from tools/ux/shot.mjs).
d. **Critique** like a harsh studio critic on an Awwwards jury. Score 0-10 each: concept, craft, typography,
   motion/interaction, fit to the app, clarity. Penalise anything templated or AI-looking. List tight, specific fixes,
   fix them, re-shoot, re-score. Stop at >= 9/10 overall or after 4 iterations. Keep a log in `PLAN.md`.
e. **Remove one accessory**: a final pass that takes out the one thing that doesn't earn its place.
f. **Report**: folder; the concept in 2 sentences; the signature moment; libraries used; final scores and the critic's
   last notes; known limitations; the path of your best rest-state screenshot.
