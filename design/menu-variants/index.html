Devoxx 2026: five main menus · the hall screen after the conference

Same content in all five: the story (the 8 verdicts and their debates), watch first, search, talks sorted by views / like rate / hearts, the programme by day and the text version. Each was built by its own agent in a screenshot-and-critique loop. Click a card for the live demo; "phone" opens the 340×690 totem version. Scores are each agent's own critic, before the cross-check.

Split-flap 9.0

The venue's departures board: the 8 verdicts are destinations, watch first, talks and programme are the same board re-flapped.

Signature: every keystroke, sort or tab re-flaps the cells letter by letter; your letters land in orange mid-flip.
Debate arena 8.7

The conference as its 8 arguments: each verdict a line between its two sides, speakers as dots, watch first and search in a rail.

Signature: point at a speaker and a thread runs to their talk and their other debates; the rest dims.
Sonar 8.6

A radar of what the talks told you to do: 46 practices on do it now / try it / watch it / push back, across agents, security, JVM and architecture.

Signature: a sweep arm you can grab and drag; practices ping as it passes and stack up in the side panel.
Reel 8.9

The week as a filmstrip: a countdown leader, the 8 verdicts as title cards, the picks as end credits.

Signature: drag the strip through the projector gate; the frame in the gate lights up and Enter projects its debate full screen.
jshell 9.0

The conference as a Java REPL: it boots with verdicts(); every chip and row types its command for you, so it works by mouse alone.

Signature: autocomplete that previews the result as you type; Tab accepts, Enter runs.
