# Creative director notes (round 2, 20/10)

## What I saw

- Now-in-hall: navy board, orange card, 13 time chips, 5 day chips, 4 cards, search box. Reads as a kiosk UI, not
  as a thing in a room. Nothing on it is big enough to read from the back of the hall.
- Round-1 jshell: a table of 8 rows plus a command bar. Honest, but a spreadsheet on a wall.
- The hall itself is quiet: pale curtain wall, two plants, ivy, a strip light. The screen is the only coloured
  thing. Whatever is on it becomes the room's mood. A bright screen would light the plants; a red one would make it
  a cinema; a blue one keeps it a lobby.
- Data worth a wall: 205 talks, 8 verdicts, 8 debates each with 2-3 sides and named people, rooms 3-10 (+BOF 1-2),
  Mon 5-Fri 9 Oct, 185 talks have a seat (walkable). Devoxx orange #F28C28-ish, navy.

## 20 concepts (long list)

1. Title cards: 8 verdicts as 8 Saul Bass cards, hard cuts, one line of type each.
2. Two voices: one question, two stances, lenticular split that follows the cursor.
3. Seat plan: 205 talks as 205 seats in a plan of the hall; sort puts the best in front.
4. Wayfinding: Aicher-style room numerals 3..10; a room is a door to its talks.
5. Lightbox: a white marquee lightbox, one enormous line, the whole screen is the search field.
6. Academy leader: countdown 8..1 with the wipe, one verdict per number.
7. End credits: the week as a cast list you scrub with the wheel.
8. Curtain: a flat red curtain parts on hover to show the programme.
9. Station clock: Hilfiker clock, 5 days as hours, stop-and-go hand.
10. Scoreboard: hand-operated baseball scoreboard, 23 : 20 : 16 ... per verdict.
11. Bookshelf: 205 spines, hover pulls one out.
12. Star chart: talks as stars in 8 constellations.
13. Punch card: a Hollerith card of the week; holes are talks.
14. Balance beam: a verdict as a beam that tilts to the side you hover.
15. Ticket stub: the programme as a cinema ticket; tear to open a day.
16. Tide chart: attention over the week as one calm line.
17. One sentence poster: the intro set as a Müller-Brockmann poster, verdict words as links.
18. Radio dial: Rams/Braun tuning dial for days and rooms.
19. Heap diagram: compact object headers as a bar of bits, the JVM story as the picture.
20. Chalk tally: 205 marks in groups of five, by verdict.

Dropped because: 6/7 overlap 1 (cinema typography); 12/20 overlap 3 (fields of marks); 8/15 are props, not
information; 9/18 make days the subject when the week's story is the subject; 10/14/16 are charts, John asked for
"look good" not "measure"; 11/13/19 are nerdy without being readable from across the hall; 17 is 80 words.

Kept: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5. Five different surfaces: red field, split field, dark plan, blue sign, white lightbox.
