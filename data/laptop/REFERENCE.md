# Dell Precision 5570: reference for the Blender model (researched 14/10/2026)

Geometry (Dell P91F manual): 344.40 x 230.14 mm, ~18 mm closed (lid ~5 mm), 2.1 kg. 15.6" 16:10, active area
336.38 x 210.24 mm. Bezels ~4 mm sides, ~5.5-6 mm top (camera, IR, sensor), chin ~4-8 mm (partly behind the deck
when open). Plan corners r ~4-6 mm, bright diamond-cut chamfer on lid and base edges. One wide centre hinge spanning
most of the rear; max opening 135 deg. Keys 19.05 x 18.05 mm pitch, block ~265 x 88 mm centred; touchpad 150.9 x 90 mm,
~10 mm below the keys, ~8 mm from the front; speaker grilles ~11 mm wide full keyboard height either side; power
button = blank top-right key (fingerprint).
Materials: lid "Titan Gray" anodised aluminium, albedo ~#9A9896, metallic, satin; Dell ring logo ~30 mm centred, polished
bright metal (Precision; the XPS's is black). Deck black woven carbon fibre, soft touch ~#2E2E30, ~2 mm weave. Keys
#1C1C1E, white legends, half-height function row. Matte black touchpad. Intel sticker bottom right of the palm rest.
Display: UHD+ glossy glass edge to edge (FHD+ matte). Battery LED on the front edge.
Same chassis as the XPS 15 9520 (darker lid, shiny logo).
Sticker: vinyl + gloss laminate ~0.15 mm, die-cut white border ~3 mm, glossier than the lid, thin edge highlight, a hint
of contact shadow, maybe one corner lifted 0.2-0.5 mm.
Photos: tomshardware.com/reviews/dell-xps-15-9500-2020 (open, top-down, closed lid); notebookcheck Precision 5560 review
(darker lid, shiny logo); Wikimedia Commons "Wikimedia_stickers_on_a_laptop_cover_01.jpg" (die-cuts on a silver lid).
